Nykaa: five journeys and three shoppers

A shopper's walkthrough of the Nykaa app across five journeys, each broken into steps tagged smooth, slow, confusing or frustrating, followed by conversations with three real users.

Journeys

#1First-time login and checking offers

Login was quick, but a profile prompt, a notification prompt and a full-screen ad stacked up before any product was visible, and the home screen was too loud. The Offers page was clean and easy to scan.

Verdict
Bad entry, good offers
Recommendation
Hold the profile and notification prompts until after the first browse, put a clear close button on every pop-up, and stop the entry ad from blocking the home screen.

#2Finding a sunscreen for oily skin

Category led back into Categories, four steps deep before any sunscreen showed. The shop page offered Shop by Formulation but not by skin type, and the skin quiz only updated a small tile. The oily filter, the product page and AI-summarised reviews worked well and closed the sale.

Verdict
Bad navigation, good product page
Recommendation
Flatten the Category menu so it does not loop, make products reachable in one or two taps, offer skin type as a filter up front, and let the quiz refresh the full results page.

#3Ordering a Cetaphil face wash

Search autocomplete, the product page and size choice were clear. After Add to Bag there was only a small toast and no path forward, and the only coupon required shopping for two more items. Auto-credited reward points helped.

Verdict
Mixed: great discovery, leaky checkout
Recommendation
Show a clear Go to Bag action after adding, offer coupons a first-time buyer can use, and showcase the reward points more.

#4Nykaa Play: content and discovery

The short-video feed loaded instantly and felt familiar. Searching for a specific shade swatch returned unrelated tutorials and ads, and there was no one-tap way to add the variant shown to the bag.

Verdict
Bad
Recommendation
Tie Nykaa Play search to the product catalog. For a specific shade, show only user-generated or official swatches of that product, with an Add to Bag button on the video.

#5Accounts and wallet

The account page was calm and clean. The wallet showed auto-credited reward points and instant-refund options up front. The Prive loyalty page was a wall of text that made the next tier hard to work out.

Verdict
Good, with a caveat
Recommendation
Make the Prive dashboard visual, with a progress bar such as spend 500 rupees more to unlock the next tier.

Key takeaways

Discovery and trust are strengthsAI-summarised reviews and clear product pages are what convert.
Onboarding and checkout leak trustStacked pop-ups, a silent add-to-cart and a coupon that does not fit the order.
Ratings divergeApp-store rating 4.7 stars against long-form review sentiment of 1.6 to 2.6 stars: delivery and support are bigger risks than any single UI flow.
Users follow convenience, not the appPeople are loyal to whatever suits the reason they open it that day.
