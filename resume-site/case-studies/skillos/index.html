SkillOS: proving training changed capability

A B2B SaaS concept for mid-market L&D teams. It runs baseline assessments, retrains through the customer's existing LMS, retests, and outputs a CFO-ready Capability Lift Report. Pricing is per covered employee.

Strategy in five parts

#1The problem

Companies train employees but cannot prove capability improved. LMS data shows completion and hours, not skill.

Evidence
87% of organisations cannot isolate training's impact (ATD); 70% of training is forgotten within weeks (McKinsey)
So what
Replace completion reports with a capability score by person, team and role.

#2Why now

Three shifts are converging: AI adoption, faster skill obsolescence and a focus on internal mobility.

Evidence
39% of core skills expected to change by 2030 (WEF, 2025); 86% of HR leaders prioritise internal mobility (LinkedIn, 2024)
So what
Launch with measurement now, while budgets are being reviewed to prove ROI.

#3Segment and market

Mid-market scores 33 out of 45 against 29 for enterprise and 25 for startups: the right need, size and timing. Early interviews with a Head of L&D, a CHRO and a CFO supported the wedge.

Evidence
TAM about 1.3 billion dollars, SAM about 160 million, SOM about 3.6 million by Year 3 (70 customers, about 2.3% of SAM)
So what
Land one 90-day pilot on a single role family, then expand to the whole workforce.

#4Solution: Measure, Develop, Predict

Year 1 measures with role-family baselines and a Capability Lift Report. Year 2 develops with gap-based recommendations and manager nudges. Year 3 predicts skill demand, role readiness and obsolescence risk.

Evidence
Workflow: assessment, capability graph, AI recommendations, insights
So what
Measurement is the wedge; the later layers build on the same longitudinal data.

#5Defensibility and go-to-market

AI alone is not the moat, since any competitor can prompt an LLM. The edge is longitudinal pre and post capability data plus deep HRIS and LMS integrations. The closest rival named is iMocha.

Evidence
Three-year vision: about 70 customers and about 3.6 million dollars ARR
So what
Per-employee subscription; prove ROI in one team, then expand company-wide.
