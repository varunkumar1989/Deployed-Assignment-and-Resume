Noon UAE: the first-time buyer journey

A new user signs up, browses, adds a product to the cart and tries to check out. The journey ends at a real unserviceable-address block, which is evaluated as part of the flow.

Findings

#1Cluttered sign-up modal

Rotating decorative icons dominate the screen, so the login fields feel secondary.

Principle
Aesthetic and minimalist design
Fix
Remove the icon carousel and make the email field and button the focus.

#2Email OTP detour

The email holds a link, not the code. The code appears on a web page and must be copied back into the app: three steps to enter one code.

Principle
Jakob's Law
Fix
Show the 6-digit code in the email body and support autofill.

#3Product page overload

Coupons, cashback, delivery options, an AI assistant, seller card, payment offers and ads compete at once.

Principle
Miller's Law
Fix
Group into collapsible sections so only one or two chunks show by default.

#4Unexplained 5.0 rating

A prominent 5.0 rating sits above a reviews panel that says there are no reviews yet.

Principle
Consistency and standards
Fix
Hide the rating until a minimum count, or label its source.

#5Cart distractions

Five recommended products, coupon prompts, cashback and installment offers surround the checkout button.

Principle
Aesthetic and minimalist design
Fix
Move cross-sell below the fold or after checkout.

#6Dead-end address error

“Outside serviceable location” offers no nearby zone, pickup point or explanation.

Principle
Help users recover from errors
Fix
Suggest the nearest covered zone or a pickup locker in the error state.

#7Small modal close buttons

Close buttons are about 24px, low contrast and far from where the cursor rests.

Principle
Fitts's Law
Fix
Enlarge hit areas to at least 40px with stronger contrast.

Redesign direction

Simplify onboardingDrop the carousel; show the OTP inline in the email.
Chunk the product pageCollapse offers, delivery and cross-sell.
Protect checkoutRemove recommendation rails from the cart.
Make errors recoverableOffer the nearest serviceable option on the same screen.
Fix cursor targetsClose buttons at 40px or more.
Earn the star ratingCaveat ratings that have no written reviews.
