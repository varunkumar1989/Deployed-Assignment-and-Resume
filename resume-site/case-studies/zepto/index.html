Zepto: grow the basket without losing frequency

Zepto's average order value has stayed near ₹388 while rivals sit at ₹547 to ₹750 or more. The goal is ₹388 to ₹480 in six months, without slowing down the one-item shopper.

Research and problem

#1Baskets are small

90% of baskets are under ₹500 and 60% of orders have 1 to 3 items.

Evidence
10 Zepto users interviewed
So what
Most orders are urgent top-ups, not planned shops.

#2Free delivery feels like a finish line

70% stop adding once free delivery is unlocked. Market data suggests about 58% add items only to cross the threshold.

Evidence
Interviews and market data
So what
The checkout moment needs a reason to keep going.

#3Users are open to more, if it fits

50% would add an item if it fits their cart, and 0% explore new categories today.

Evidence
Interviews
So what
Relevance matters more than discounts.

#4Four shopper segments

Urgency Grabbers 40%, Impulse Snackers 20%, Planned Top-Uppers 30%, Deal seekers 10%. Top-Uppers show bigger baskets already happen.

Evidence
Segmentation of the 10 users
So what
Design for the 60% who shop small without hurting the others.

#5Core problem: built to finish fast

Search is literal (chips never shows salsa or dip), suggestions are generic bestsellers, and one item in cart feels like the job is done. Speed, Zepto's strength, is also the blocker.

Evidence
5 whys root-cause analysis
So what
The barrier is missing relevance at the right moment, not price.

Three fixes, prioritised with RICE

Zepto Surprise Box · RICE 105 · nowA ₹49 or ₹99 curated box worth more than its price, offered after free delivery. Never pre-added. Lowest effort and brand-funded.
Smart Deal Shelf · RICE 77 · nextA short shelf of cart-matched, brand-funded deals above Pay. Always skippable, no countdown timer. +₹40 to 100 per order.
AI Shopkeeper · RICE 45 · laterSay pizza night for 4 and get a full, editable cart in one tap, aiming for 8 to 12 items. Sponsored picks labelled.
North star and guardrailsAOV ₹388 to ₹480, plus items per order and attach rate. Order frequency must not fall, checkout under 2 minutes, launch to 5% first and roll back if frequency dips.
