Uber: solving the last-100-metre pickup problem

Where the app drops a pin and where the driver stands often differ in malls, airports, tech parks and gated communities. This study follows Assumptions, Hypotheses, Evidence, Validated insights.

What the research found

#1Riders are often unsure where to wait

About 75% of surveyed riders wander searching for their ride at least sometimes, and about 50% report unexpectedly long waits.

Evidence
Survey, n=25, Bengaluru
Implication
Pickup confusion is routine, not a rare edge case.

#2A passive pin does not recover a lost pickup

All surveyed riders phone the driver once the driver cannot find them. Long wait is the top stated cancellation reason.

Evidence
Survey, n=25; interview coding
Implication
A shared meet-here signal is needed alongside better positioning.

#3Multi-exit buildings fail repeatedly

Towers and malls with two or more gates default the pin to the wrong one. Malls account for 44% of confusion locations, office parks 32% and airports 16%.

Evidence
Survey and interviews
Implication
Exit-specific pickup guidance for dense venues.

#4It is not only a GPS problem

The hypothesis that pin accuracy alone would fix most complaints was only partially confirmed: positioning is the technical root, and human coordination is a second gap.

Evidence
Interview coding and competitor teardown
Implication
Fix positioning and coordination together.

#5Nobody has fixed the everyday, un-zoned pickup

Airport zones and PIN dispatch cover the airport case. Competitors in India and abroad have not shipped a general-purpose fix for malls, tech parks, hospitals and gates.

Evidence
Competitor teardown of ten companies
Implication
The opportunity sits in everyday pickups outside designated zones.

Impact and context

Modeled impactAbout 1 in 6 rides in the affected segment cancelled over pickup confusion, and about 86 million dollars a year in modeled recoverable value. These are research-team estimates, not Uber data.
Who it affectsTech-park commuters, mall visitors, airport travellers and first-time riders.
Market contextRapido's monthly users overtook Uber and Ola in early 2026, and 2025 aggregator rules put a cost on cancellations.
ComplianceLive location is personal data under India's DPDP Act, so any feature must be consent-clean.
